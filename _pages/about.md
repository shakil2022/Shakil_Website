I am a machine learning researcher and <a href='https://bubt.edu.bd/department/department-of-computer-science-engineering/faculty/profile/MDSHA'>Lecturer</a> in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT), currently completed my M.Sc. in <a href='https://iitju.edu.bd/'>Information and Communication Technology at the Institute of Information Technology, Jahangirnagar University.</a>. My research interests lie at the intersection of fFederated Learning, Cyber Security, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Image Processing, Computer Vision.

My current research focuses on developing privacy-preserving, data-efficient, and reliable machine learning systems, particularly for healthcare applications. As part of my M.Sc. research, I am working on federated semi-supervised learning for Parkinson’s disease classification, with an emphasis on learning effectively from limited labeled data while preserving data privacy. This work has been supported by the <a href='https://ims.ictd.gov.bd/'>ICT Division Fellowship 2025–2026,</a> with the research project titled FEP-SSL: A Privacy-Preserving Semi-Supervised Learning Framework for Parkinson’s Disease Classification. I have also been awarded the <a href='https://grant.most.gov.bd/en/services/most/nst-fellowship'>National Science and Technology (NST) Fellowship 2025–2026 </a> for research on Privacy Preserving Federated Semi-Supervised Learning Model for Healthcare Applications.

My research journey began with computer vision and deep learning, including work on real-time Bangla Sign Language detection and recognition using YOLOv10 and medical image analysis. Under the supervision and guidance of <a href='https://juniv.edu/teachers/mskaiser'>Professor Dr. M  Shamim Kaiser</a> at the Institute of Information Technology, Jahangirnagar University, I have expanded my research toward privacy-preserving, explainable, and data-efficient machine learning, with a particular focus on federated learning and healthcare AI. My current research includes Cleanlab-guided uncertainty-based active learning for Parkinson’s disease classification and privacy-preserving federated semi-supervised learning, aiming to address the challenges of limited labeled data and privacy-sensitive healthcare applications.

Alongside research, I am passionate about teaching and mentoring students in computer science and engineering. I have worked as a <a href='https://bubt.edu.bd/'>Lecturer at Bangladesh University of Business and Technology (BUBT)</a>, <a href='https://www.cub.edu.bd/'>Canadian University of Bangladesh (CUB)</a>, and <a href='https://daffodilvarsity.edu.bd/'>Daffodil International University (DIU)</a> and previously served as a Teaching Assistant and Research Assistant at <a href='https://juniv.edu/'>Jahangirnagar University</a>.
